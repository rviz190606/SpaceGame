# SpaceGame

A simple 2D arcade shooter built with C++ and SFML.  
Your mission: survive waves of enemy planes and score as high as possible.

---

## Gameplay

- **Controls**
  - Left / Right Arrow Keys → Move left or right
  - Up / Down Arrow Keys → Limited vertical movement (small range)
  - Spacebar → Fire bullets

- **Enemies**
  - **Normal Plane**
    - Fires targeted bullets toward your position at the time of firing
    - Destroying one gives +100 points
  - **Bomber Plane**
    - Drops bombs that explode on impact
    - Destroying one gives +200 points

- **Difficulty Scaling**
  - Every 300 points:
    - Enemy planes move faster
    - Enemy fire rate increases
    - Your plane’s movement speed also increases

- **Objective**
  - Shoot down as many planes as possible
  - Avoid enemy bullets and bombs
  - Increase your score as much as possible

---

## Features

- Fast-paced arcade gameplay
- Increasing difficulty for endless challenge
- Two enemy types with distinct behaviors
- Background music and sound effects for shooting
- Score tracking and game-over screen with restart option

---

## Installation & Running

### Requirements
- SFML 2.6

### Installation
- To create the executable run 
```bash
make
```
